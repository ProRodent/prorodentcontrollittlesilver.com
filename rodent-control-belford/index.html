Belford is part of Middletown Township.

Belford does not have its own borough hall or mayor. It is an unincorporated section of Middletown Township, so calls from this area are handled through the township, not a separate town government. That does not change how we cover the ZIP: 07718 is inside our service area, and a call from Belford gets the same response as any other town on our list.

We are based in Little Silver, not in Belford, so there is no local office or standing crew here. What we offer is a line that answers calls from this ZIP and technicians who route out to handle them.

What we handle on calls from this area.

Most calls fall into a few groups. Some residents hear scratching in an attic at night and want it checked before it gets worse. Others find droppings along a baseboard or in a garage and want to know what left them. A number of calls come in about a smell that will not go away, which is often a sign of a dead rodent inside a wall or duct.

We also get calls about rodents that got into a parked car, chewing at wiring or insulation under the hood. If that sounds like your situation, our vehicle and car rodent removal page covers how that visit works. For a general mouse problem, mouse control explains our trap-and-seal approach in more detail.

How a visit here typically goes.

A technician starts by walking the property, inside and out, to find where rodents are getting in and where they are active. That inspection sets the plan for the rest of the visit.

  • Look at entry points around the foundation, utility lines, and garage or crawl space openings.
  • Set traps or stations where activity is concentrated, based on droppings and gnaw marks.
  • Seal gaps with materials rodents cannot chew through, not just foam or steel wool alone.
  • Schedule a follow-up to check catches and confirm the sealing is holding.

Exclusion work for Belford homes.

Trapping alone rarely finishes the job if the entry point stays open. Our rodent exclusion service closes the gaps that let rodents back in, which matters most in older homes near the water where framing has settled over time. If damage has already happened, such as chewed insulation or gnawed wood, we can also give you an estimate through our rodent damage repair page.

Recurring activity is common when the first fix only removes the rodents already inside without closing the gap they used. If you have had traps set before and the problem came back within a season, tell us that history when you call. It changes how thoroughly we check the exterior and which materials we use to close the gap for good.

Garages and basements often get overlooked because people focus on the living space first. A garage with a gap under the door or a basement with an open sump area can host a nest for weeks before anyone notices it indoors. If either area sounds familiar, our garage and basement rodent control page covers that work specifically.

When an emergency visit makes sense.

Not every call needs to be treated as urgent, but some do. Rodents actively chewing wiring, a nest found close to a stove or furnace, or a family member with a strong reaction to droppings are all reasons to say so clearly. Call the 24/7 line and we will prioritize those situations against the regular schedule.

What to expect on the call.

  • Tell us your ZIP code and cross street so we can route the visit correctly.
  • Describe what you saw or heard, and roughly when.
  • Mention any pets or small children in the home so we can plan treatment safely.
  • Ask about timing. The line is open 24/7, and same-day visits happen when the schedule allows.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from any area with droppings or a suspected nest.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings. That can spread dust into the air.
  • Do not set out loose bait or poison on your own before we arrive.
  • Note where you saw or heard activity, and roughly what time of day.
  • Clear access to the attic, garage, or crawl space if you can do so safely.

Questions about Belford, NJ

Do you cover Belford, NJ?

Yes. Belford's 07718 ZIP code is in our coverage area, about 6 miles from our Little Silver base.

Is emergency service available in Belford?

The line is open 24/7. Same-day visits happen when the schedule allows, so call and tell us what you're seeing.

Is Belford its own town with a local government?

No. Belford is a section of Middletown Township, not a separate municipality, so it does not have its own borough hall or mayor.

How do I know if it's mice or rats?

Droppings size is one clue. Mouse droppings are small and pointed, while rat droppings are larger and blunt. We confirm the species during the inspection so the plan fits what's actually there.

What if the smell is coming from inside a wall?

That usually means a dead rodent decomposing where it cannot be easily reached. We locate the source, remove it, and treat the area so the smell clears rather than just masking it.

Can you handle a nest found in a car engine?

Yes, that's a common call, especially when a car sits unused for a few days. We check the engine bay and nearby structures where the nest may have started.

Do you use bait that's safe around pets?

Tell us about any pets when you call. We can plan trap and station placement to keep them away from areas your animals can reach.
