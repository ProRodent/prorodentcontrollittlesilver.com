Covering Deal's 07723 ZIP code.

Deal is an incorporated borough with its own local government, part of Monmouth County along the Jersey Shore. We do not keep a local office or standing crew in town. Our base is in Little Silver, and our line answers calls from Deal along with the other towns in our coverage list.

Coastal towns like this often have older housing stock close to the water, and that age and proximity can create more gaps around foundations and utility lines than a newer inland home. We do not claim to know your specific house, but that general pattern is common along the shore.

Common reasons people call about rodents here.

A lot of calls start with droppings found in a garage, basement, or kitchen cabinet. Others start with a sound: scratching in the ceiling at night, or scurrying behind a wall. Some callers are dealing with a mixed problem, mice and ants at the same time, which is more common than people expect.

We also hear from people getting a house ready to sell or rent, sometimes through a real estate agent calling on the seller's behalf, who want a cleanup done before showings start. If that's your situation, our attic and crawl space rodent cleanup page covers what that visit looks like.

How we approach a visit in Deal.

Every visit starts with a walkthrough, not a guess. We look at where droppings, gnaw marks, or grease trails are concentrated, and we check likely entry points outside before doing anything indoors.

  • Inspect the attic, basement or crawl space, and exterior foundation line.
  • Place traps or tamper-resistant stations based on what the inspection shows.
  • Seal openings with materials that hold up to gnawing, not just foam.
  • Return to check results and confirm the sealing is still intact.

If the job includes rebuilding chewed trim, insulation, or wiring covers, we can walk through that separately under our rodent damage repair service. For rats specifically, including outdoor burrows near a shed or deck, see rat control.

Before you call, a few things help.

Knowing roughly where the activity is and how long it's been going on helps us plan the visit before we even arrive. We'll ask about pets, kids, and whether anyone in the home has specific concerns about treatment methods.

Businesses and larger properties.

Deal has a mix of year-round homes and seasonal properties, and some calls come from small businesses along the main commercial stretches. Commercial spaces often need a different placement plan than a house, since foot traffic and food storage areas change where rodents are likely to travel. Our commercial rodent control page covers what that kind of visit involves.

Vacant or seasonal homes can also build up more activity than an occupied house simply because nobody is there to notice the early signs. If a property has sat empty for a stretch, mention that when you call, since it changes what we expect to find at the first inspection and how much cleanup the visit may involve.

When rodents are already gone but the damage is left behind, chewed wiring covers, gnawed baseboards, or torn insulation, we can fold a repair estimate into the same visit rather than sending you elsewhere for that work.

Whatever the property type, the same basic rule applies: an open gap will keep letting rodents in no matter how many are trapped. Sealing is what turns a one-time fix into a lasting one, and we treat it as a standard part of the visit rather than an upsell added later.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children out of any room where you've seen droppings or heard activity.
  • Do not sweep dry droppings. Ventilate the area and wear gloves if you need to be in that room.
  • Do not put out your own loose bait before the visit.
  • Write down where and when you noticed activity, even roughly.
  • Clear a path to the attic hatch, crawl space, or garage if it's safe to do so.
