What we handle for calls from this area.

Most calls we get from this part of the county are straightforward: mice or rats seen in the house, or droppings found and no clear sense of how bad the problem is. We inspect first in every case, because guessing at severity over the phone rarely matches what a technician finds in person.

We also hear regularly from people dealing with a nest, sometimes with young found in a specific spot like a garage corner or under a shed, and from people who want a home sealed after repeat activity rather than treated again and again. That sealing work is covered on our rodent exclusion page. A smaller but steady share of calls involve odor with no visible animal, which our dead rodent removal and odor control service is built to handle.

Leonardo is part of Middletown Township.

Leonardo is an unincorporated section of Middletown Township, not its own separate borough or city. There is no Leonardo town hall or mayor; local government runs through Middletown. We are based in Little Silver, about 6 miles away, and Leonardo's 07737 ZIP falls within our regular coverage, not a separate branch office.

How the visit itself is run.

The inspection comes first and it is not a formality. A technician looks at the whole property, not just the room you called about, since rodents often move between rooms through wall voids and framing gaps. Our rodent inspection and estimate page walks through what that step covers.

After the inspection we set traps or stations on the routes we actually find, not just around the house at random. Sealing follows once trapping is underway, closing the gaps that let rodents in with materials they cannot chew through. We come back to check the traps and confirm activity has stopped before the job is closed.

Not sure this matches what you are seeing? Describe it on the call and we will help you sort it out.

A note on garages, sheds and vehicles.

We get a fair number of calls about rodents found in a parked vehicle, often after someone notices chewed wiring or a build-up of nesting material near the engine bay. That is a distinct kind of job, and our vehicle and car rodent removal page explains what it involves. Detached garages and sheds get the same inspection treatment as the main house, since they often have their own entry points.

Pet-safe treatment when it matters.

A good number of calls come from households with a dog or cat, and people reasonably want to know the treatment will not put a pet at risk. We do not rely on loose poison for that reason, since a pet can find it before a rodent does. Traps and enclosed stations placed along known travel routes keep the treatment targeted rather than scattered.

When timing matters.

Some calls are less about the rodents themselves and more about a deadline. A pre-move-out cleanup ahead of a closing, or a real estate agent calling on behalf of a seller, both come up from time to time, and we try to schedule around those dates when we can.

Cleanup after an infestation clears.

Once activity has been treated, some households want help cleaning insulation, sealing the last small gaps, or getting an estimate on repairs to chewed wiring or ductwork. Our rodent damage repair page covers that kind of follow-up work, and our attic and crawl space rodent cleanup service handles the mess an established nest can leave behind.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from any area with droppings or activity.
  • Note where and roughly when you saw or heard signs of rodents.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings.
  • Do not put out loose bait or poison before we arrive.
  • Clear access to the attic, garage or crawl space if that is where the activity is.
