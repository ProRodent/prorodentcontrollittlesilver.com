How a visit works.

We start with an inspection, checking the interior and exterior for droppings, gnaw marks, grease trails and entry gaps. Then we treat the active problem with traps and, where it fits, tamper-resistant stations kept away from children and pets.

After that, we seal the gaps we found using materials rodents cannot chew through, such as hardware cloth and sheet metal, and not steel wool alone. We return to check the traps and stations and confirm the activity has stopped. If a vehicle is involved, our vehicle rodent removal service covers engine bays and cabin checks separately from house work.

Notes for shore-adjacent properties.

Like other towns near the water that we cover, Manasquan can see rats pushed toward higher ground after storms or high tide, and mice moving indoors as fall nights cool. These are general coastal patterns, not specific claims about any one Manasquan street.

If your property is close to the water or has had recent flooding, mention it on the call. It changes where we check first outside and how we plan sealing work around the foundation.

Garages, sheds and outdoor storage.

Outdoor storage, sheds and detached garages are common in Manasquan, and they give rodents a quiet place to nest away from daily foot traffic. Stored boxes, firewood and lawn equipment can all hide a nest for weeks before anyone notices droppings or chewed material.

Our backyard and outdoor rodent control service covers sheds, decks and yard structures separately from the main house, so a full property check includes those spaces rather than stopping at the back door.

If you keep a boat, trailer or garden shed near the house, mention it on the call. Rodents often nest in equipment that sits unused for stretches of time, and checking those spots early can catch a small problem before it spreads to the house itself.

Combined pest calls come up here too, where mice show up alongside ants or another insect issue around the same time. We handle the rodent side directly and can point you to the right next step for anything outside that scope, so one call still gets you a clear answer.

What to tell us on the call.

  • Where you noticed activity, and roughly when.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from the area with activity.
  • Note where and when you noticed droppings, chewing or noise.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings.
  • Clear access to the attic, garage or crawl space if you can.
