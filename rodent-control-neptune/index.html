Coverage across Neptune Township.

Neptune is a township in Monmouth County, and its 07753 ZIP code falls inside our regular service area, about 8.8 miles from our Little Silver base. Township government here spans a wide footprint, including the Ocean Grove section, which is its own unincorporated part of the township rather than a separate town government.

Because a township can stretch across many streets, tell us the nearest cross street when you call. That detail helps us route the visit and gives a technician a clear sense of where the property sits inside the township.

What Neptune callers ask us to handle.

Most calls follow a handful of patterns. Some residents hear scratching in an attic at night and want it checked before it spreads. Others find droppings in a kitchen drawer or garage shelf and want the mess cleaned up safely and the source found.

We also hear from people who found a small nest with young in a shed or storage bin, and from drivers whose vehicle wiring was chewed after a car sat parked for a while. A vehicle rodent removal visit and a mouse control visit start from the same kind of inspection, though the fix looks different once we know the animal and the location.

A smaller share of calls come from a real estate agent handling a property ahead of a sale, or from a homeowner who wants entry points sealed before the weather turns colder.

How a Neptune visit runs.

A visit starts with a walk of the property, inside and out, to find where rodents are getting in and how far the activity has spread. We look at rooflines, vents, foundation edges and any gaps near pipes or wiring.

Once we know what we are dealing with, we place traps or tamper-resistant stations along the paths rodents actually use, not just wherever is convenient. Then we close the entry points with materials that hold up to chewing, and we schedule a follow-up to confirm the activity has stopped.

If a lingering smell is the reason for the call, our dead rodent removal and odor control visit locates the animal, removes it and treats the area so the odor fades faster than it would on its own.

Homes and small businesses inside the township.

Neptune Township includes stretches of retail and small business as well as residential streets, so we take calls from shop owners as well as homeowners. A commercial rodent control visit follows the same inspect, treat, seal and follow-up order, adjusted for a business's hours and storage areas.

Why fall brings more calls.

Cooling nights from September through November push mice toward warm buildings, and that pattern holds across Monmouth County, including Neptune Township. Garages, sheds and homes with gaps around utility lines tend to see the first arrivals of the season.

If you have had activity before, this is also a good time to check the exterior for new gaps before the cold sets in. A brief exclusion check now can prevent a bigger call in December.

Standard homeowners insurance policies commonly exclude damage caused by rodents, so it is worth reading your policy or asking your agent before assuming repairs are covered. Anyone hiring a pest control provider in New Jersey can also ask for their license number and confirm it with the NJDEP Pesticide Control Program before work begins.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from the area where you saw or heard activity.
  • Note the room, the time of day, and what you saw or heard.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings.
  • Do not set out loose bait or poison on your own.
  • Clear access to the attic hatch, crawl space or garage corner if you can.

Is emergency service available in Neptune?

How do I know if it's mice or rats?

Mouse droppings are small and pointed, while rat droppings run larger and blunt. Rats also tend to burrow outside near foundations and sheds. Describe what you see and we can narrow it down before the visit.

Can you clean up droppings safely?

Yes. We ventilate the area, wear gloves, wet the droppings with disinfectant before removal and bag the waste. We do not recommend sweeping or vacuuming them dry.

Is treatment safe around pets?

We use tamper-resistant bait stations placed out of reach of pets, children and wildlife, and we explain what is placed and where before we leave.

Do you handle rodents found in a car?

Yes. Rodents nesting in an engine bay or cabin over a parked stretch is a common call, and our vehicle rodent removal service checks the vehicle and treats nearby harborage.

Do you work with real estate agents before a sale?

Yes. Agents sometimes call ahead of a listing or closing to have a property inspected and cleared. Give us the address and the timeline and we will work with it.
