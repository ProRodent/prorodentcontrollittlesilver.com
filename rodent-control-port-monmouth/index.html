What we handle for Port Monmouth callers.

A good share of calls from this area start with rodents in an attic or wall void, either scratching heard at night or droppings found later. Others call about a nest, sometimes with a mother and babies, found in a specific spot like a garage corner or under a deck.

Because this is a waterfront section, we also hear more calls about rats than in some inland towns, including burrows near sheds or foundations. Our rat control page covers how we approach that species specifically, and backyard and outdoor rodent control covers burrows and outdoor activity.

Port Monmouth is a section of Middletown Township.

Port Monmouth does not have its own municipal government. It is an unincorporated section of Middletown Township, so there is no separate borough hall or mayor here. That doesn't affect our coverage: the 07758 ZIP is on our list, and calls from here are routed the same as any other town we serve.

We're based in Little Silver, roughly 7 miles away, and we don't have a standing office or crew in Port Monmouth itself. What we provide is phone coverage and technicians who travel out to handle the visit.

Inspect, treat, seal, follow up.

We use the same general process across every town we cover, adjusted to what the inspection finds at your address.

  • Walk the interior and exterior to find where rodents are getting in and where they're active.
  • Set traps or stations in the spots the inspection points to, not just a standard set.
  • Close entry points with materials rodents can't chew through.
  • Come back to check results and make sure the sealing held up.

Garages, sheds and vehicles near the water.

Outbuildings close to the water tend to draw more outdoor rodent activity than a house sits set back from it, since sheds and detached garages offer cover and rarely get checked as often as the living space. Stored firewood, tarps, and equipment give rodents places to nest undisturbed for weeks.

Vehicles parked outdoors for long stretches, whether a car, boat trailer, or utility trailer, can also become a nesting spot, especially once the weather cools and rodents look for a warm engine bay. If you notice chewed wiring or insulation under a hood, our vehicle and car rodent removal page covers how we handle that kind of call.

For outdoor burrows near a foundation, deck, or shed, our backyard and outdoor rodent control service treats the area outside before it becomes an indoor problem. Catching outdoor activity early is often easier than waiting for rodents to find their way inside.

If you're not sure whether what you're dealing with is indoors, outdoors, or both, describe what you've seen as clearly as you can when you call. We can usually narrow it down before the visit and bring the right equipment for either setting.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from any spot with droppings or a suspected nest.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread dust into the air.
  • Skip loose bait or poison on your own before we get there.
  • Note roughly where and when you noticed activity.
  • Clear access to the attic, garage, or shed if it's safe to do so.
