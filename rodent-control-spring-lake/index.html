How we handle a Spring Lake visit.

Every visit we run, in Spring Lake or anywhere else on our list, starts with an inspection. We check attics, basements, garages and the exterior for droppings, gnaw marks and the gaps rodents use to get in, and we note what we find before touching anything else.

From there we treat the active problem, usually with traps and, when the site calls for it, tamper-resistant stations placed out of reach of children and pets. Once activity has slowed, we close the entry points with materials rodents cannot chew through, and we schedule a follow-up to confirm the traps and stations have stopped catching. Larger sealing jobs are covered under our rodent exclusion service.

What Spring Lake callers ask about.

Calls here cover the same range we see across the shore: mice or rats in general, droppings that need to be cleaned up safely, and scratching in an attic or wall, especially at night. Requests for a pet-safe approach come up often, since many households have a dog or cat that uses the yard or garage.

We also take calls about rodent damage, like chewed baseboards or wiring, where people want an estimate for the repair as well as the removal. Our rodent damage repair and dead rodent removal and odor control pages cover two of the more specific jobs we handle.

Coastal patterns worth mentioning.

Cooling fall nights push mice toward warm buildings across the Jersey Shore, and storms or high tide can flood low areas and push rats toward higher ground, including sheds, decks and garages. This is a general pattern for coastal towns, not a specific claim about any one Spring Lake block.

If your property is close to the water or sits low relative to the street, mention it when you call. It affects where we check first outside.

Commercial calls near the boardwalk area.

Shore boroughs often have small shops, restaurants and rental properties mixed in among homes, especially in a busy season. Commercial buildings face many of the same pressures as houses, food storage, trash areas and utility gaps, so the inspection approach is similar even though the plan may differ.

If you manage a rental property or a small business in Spring Lake, our commercial rodent control service covers ongoing checks as well as a single response to an active problem. Tell us which kind of property you have when you call so we can plan accordingly.

Is treatment safe for a dog or cat in the yard?

How do storms affect rodent activity near the shore?

Flooding can push rats out of burrows toward higher ground, sometimes into garages, sheds or crawl spaces. If you had recent flooding, mention it when you call.

What if I only smell something, without seeing droppings?

A lingering odor, especially in a wall or crawl space, often points to a dead animal rather than an active nest. We track the smell to its source before deciding on next steps.

Do you repair chewed wiring or baseboards?

How long does a Spring Lake visit take?
